## About the Project

**Break the UTA** is a live, multiplayer icebreaker and challenge game designed to bring people together, encourage interaction, and foster exploration at campus events. Inspired by live quiz games, scavenger hunts, and speed networking, Break the UTA replaces typical passive icebreakers with real-world, interactive challenges.

### How It Works
1. **Game Setup:** An event host creates a game, customized with tailored challenges, point values, and a time limit, and distributes a unique join code.
2. **Interactive Gameplay:** Once the game goes live, participants complete real-world challenges and submit proof via the app:
   * **Meet Someone:** Connect with assigned participants, learn three facts about them, and verify the interaction.
   * **Photo Challenges:** Capture and upload photos of campus landmarks, event booths, or specific items.
   * **Prompts & Questions:** Answer questions about the hosting organization, attendees, or event themes.
   * **Custom Activities:** Complete unique instructions specified by the host.
3. **Live Competition:** Host-approved submissions award points, continuously updating a live leaderboard until time expires.

### Flexible Challenge Builder
Built for versatility, Break the UTA features a dynamic Challenge Builder allowing student organizations, club fairs, hackathons, and orientation teams to configure custom response types, point tiers, and verification requirements without changing the codebase. 

By turning the initial awkwardness of new events into an engaging, gamified experience, Break the UTA gives every attendee a reason to talk, explore, and connect.
